## Voice-over (plain language)

### What we sell
This is MindSuite.  
We sell cybersecurity services for companies.

Here is the problem we solve.

### The problem
In many companies, the board and the security team do not share the same picture.  
Directors fund one story.  
Operators live another.  
Often with two different vendors.  
Gaps stay open, and you pay twice for the same work.

### Our answer
MindSuite puts the board and the SOC on the same picture.  
One practice, one language, and one path from risk to recovery.

### What you get
That practice covers five jobs.  
Assess. Know what can hurt you.  
Protect. Guard the systems that keep the business running.  
Govern. Give the board a program it can hire and fund.  
Expose. Find what is open, and close it.  
Recover. Keep running through an attack.  
You do not pay two teams for the same work.

### Assess
First, we assess.  
We map how critical systems can be abused, and we test whether defenses hold.  
Threat modeling for what matters most.  
Intelligence both the board and the SOC can use.  
A red team that copies a real attacker, not a louder scan.  
A pen test with a clear scope.

### Protect
Then we protect the systems that keep the business running.  
Networks and endpoints.  
Cloud and containers.  
Data that must not leak.  
Who can sign in.  
Factory and plant systems.  
AI tools already in use.  
Access that is verified, not assumed.

### Govern
Then we govern.  
Policies, risk, audits, and a fractional security leader the board can hire.  
One evidence pack. A risk list directors can fund.

### Expose
Then we find what is open.  
We rank what matters.  
We close it.  
Finding gaps is not the same as protecting a laptop.  
Application security sits with the product team.  
Owners, tickets, and retests, so findings actually close.

### Recover
When an attack hits, minutes matter.  
We contain it with a retainer and playbooks.  
We investigate without confusing the watch floor.  
We rehearse ransomware before the note arrives.  
Security and IT share the same restore plan.

### Who it is for
This is for organizations that cannot afford a split picture.  
Banks. Hospitals. Factories. Product companies. Retail. Public services.  
Strategy beside operations. One SOC, twenty-four hours.
